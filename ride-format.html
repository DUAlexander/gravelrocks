How it works

It's an enduro format: you ride the full route, but only the marked timed segments count towards your result. Friday's night stage — "Gravel Rocks in the Dark" — gets the weekend started, then Saturday's longer route adds the bulk of the segments. Your segment times combine for the overall podium. Prefer not to race? Ride the whole thing at your own pace and enjoy the estate.

The stages

Friday night — Gravel Rocks in the Dark
  • ~10km, lit route across the estate
  • Timed segments count towards the overall
  • Head torches or bike lights required
Saturday — the main stage
  • ~85km across gravel estate roads
  • Multiple timed segments on route
  • One feed station
