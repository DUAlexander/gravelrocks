How it works

Like the ride, it's an enduro format — you cover the full route, but the marked timed segments decide the result. Friday's night stage opens the weekend, then choose your Saturday distance. Times combine for the overall podium, or run it purely for the experience and the festival at the end.

The stages

Friday night — Gravel Rocks in the Dark
  • ~6km, lit trail route
  • Timed segments count towards the overall
  • Head torches required
Saturday — choose your distance
  • 10, 25 or 35km across estate and moor
  • Timed segments on each route
  • One feed station on the longer routes
