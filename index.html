Ride or run — you decide

Ride
Two days of gravel, enduro-style: a Friday night stage and an 85km Saturday, with timed segments that combine for the podium. Or just ride it for the fun of it.

More than a race — it's a festival

Your entry includes two nights' camping and the whole festival: food, bar and barista coffee, a brand expo, athlete talks, and live music into the evening. Bring the family — supporters come free.
