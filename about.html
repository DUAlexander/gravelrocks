About

A two-day gravel and trail festival on the Duncombe Park estate, brought to you by Focal Events.

Our story

Gravel Rocks was built around a simple idea: bring riders and runners together for a weekend that's part race, part festival. The enduro format lets you push hard on the timed segments or just enjoy the route, and the festival — camping, food, music and the expo — is what turns a day out into a proper weekend away. It's a Focal Events production, from the team behind the Dirty Reiver.

Sustainability

We're proud members of 1% for the Planet, and we work hard to tread lightly on the estate and the wider North York Moors — recycling where we can, reducing single-use plastic, and leaving the site as we found it. We ask everyone joining us to help look after this special place.
